## Global rules seen so far
- Cadence: frames with f%3==2 repeat the previous frame (20 fps look on a 30 fps timeline).
  `shown = f - (f % 3 === 2 ? 1 : 0)`.
- Counter formula: `Math.floor(shown * 77105 / 30)`. Re-verified: f128→326,411 (held from f127),
  f129→331,551, f130/131→334,121, f176→449,779, f240→616,840, f252→647,682.
- Paper background (#E9E5DB-ish), faint grid, warm vignette. Static throughout f0–255.
- Exit style for a whole scene layer: defocus blur + fade over ~2–4 frames.
- Entry style for cutout objects: ghost frame (pale, low opacity) then solid on the next shown frame.
- Big-number entry style: per-character rise from behind a baseline clip mask, ~1 frame stagger.
